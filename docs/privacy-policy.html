Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

Last updated: October 2026

Expense Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • All categories and expenses you enter are stored locally on your device only, in a local database.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs are included.
  • No account, sign-in, or personal information is required or collected.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data from your device.

The one network request this app makes

If you log an expense in a currency other than your default one, the app looks up that day's exchange rate from frankfurter.app, a free public service backed by European Central Bank reference rates. That request contains only the two currency codes and a date (e.g. "USD to EUR on 2026-03-01") — never your amount, category, notes, or any other identifying information. If you only ever use one currency, the app makes no network requests at all.

This app is open source. You can review exactly what it does at github.com/guidohu/expensetracker.

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
