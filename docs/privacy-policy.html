Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Expense Tracker does not collect personal data, and nothing you enter ever leaves your device.

  • All categories, expenses and wishlist items you enter are stored locally on your device only, in a local database.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs are included.
  • No account, sign-in, or personal information is required or collected.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data from your device.

The network requests this app makes

The app can go online in two situations, both triggered by something you do. It never contacts any server run by the developer, and it sends nothing else.

  • Exchange rates. If you log an expense in a currency other than your default one, the app looks up that day's exchange rate from frankfurter.app, a free public service backed by European Central Bank reference rates. That request contains only the two currency codes and a date (e.g. "USD to EUR on 2026-03-01") — never your amount, category, notes, or any other identifying information.
  • Wishlist link previews. If you add a web link to your wishlist (for example by sharing a product page from your browser), the app fetches that page, and its preview image, directly from the website the link points to, to show a title and picture. That website sees the request like any other page visit, including your device's IP address. The app sends it no information about you or your expenses.

If you only use one currency and never add links to your wishlist, the app makes no network requests at all.

This app is open source. You can review exactly what it does at github.com/guidohu/expensetracker.

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
